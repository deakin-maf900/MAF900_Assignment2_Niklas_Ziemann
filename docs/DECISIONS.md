# Decisions that required judgement

Every choice below changes a number somewhere in the tables or figures. Each one names the script
that implements it, the alternative, and what the alternative does to the result where that was
measured. "Aug" marks effects measured in August 2026 on the 13-year panel with the ASX directory
of 6 August, before the R pipeline existed; they have not been re-measured in R.

|D23|**A company that files under a new name is still treated as the same company across the panel.** The last nine digits of the register's own identifier (the ACN inside the ABN) bridge the names: every row of an identifier takes the name key of its latest reporting year, and the rows before 2013-14, which carry no identifier, take the latest key of the same old name|`04`|one company per filed name (both earlier builds); a hand-written rename table|97 renames are found and printed, among them BHP Billiton to BHP Group, Woodside Petroleum to Woodside Energy, Caltex to Ampol, Incitec Pivot to Dyno Nobel, Transpacific to Cleanaway, Adelaide Brighton to Adbri. The script checks that no old name points at two current names and prints the result; it is empty. The check that it cannot make is the reverse one, two different companies reaching the same current name, and two such cases were found: the register files inconsistent identifiers for Figela Pty Ltd (ABN 64303782037 to 2016-17, then ACN 003179986) and for NSW Electricity Networks Operations (609169959, but 003179986 in 2020-21), so 15 rows of three legal entities collapse onto one key; FMR Investments files three identifiers over the panel and lands on the key "A C N 110 934 891", which is the name the register itself printed for it in 2020-21. A third defect is structural rather than a data problem: the rename is applied in a single pass, so a chain is not resolved. FMR Investments is both an old key and another entity's latest key, so Trustee for FMR Unit Trust lands on FMR Investments (1 row) while FMR Investments itself moves on to "A C N 110 934 891" (11 rows), and one entity ends up under two keys. A hand-written table would need maintaining and could not be checked against anything|

## Things that were tried and dropped

* Fuzzy name matching (D2).
* A country-of-incorporation filter on the security master. The first two builds required Compustat's
`fic` to be "AUS" alongside the exchange and the currency. That removed Rio Tinto Limited, an
Australian company trading on the ASX in Australian dollars and the fourth-largest listed emitter
of 2023-24, because Compustat carries the line under the dual-listed group; Amcor and Fletcher
Building went the same way. What defines an ASX line is the exchange and the currency it trades in. The
country-of-incorporation code added nothing to that and cost 186 lines, so it was dropped.
* A hand-built map of subsidiaries to their listed parents. Two cases are clean (APA Infrastructure
Limited under APA Group, 1.9 Mt in 2023-24; Coronado Australia Holdings under Coronado Global
Resources, 0.9 Mt) and the rest need an ownership judgement per row and a rule for unconsolidated
joint ventures such as BM Alliance Coal, so nothing was built. Named as a next step in the report.
* The vendor's `valuedate` as an event date: the vendor stamps one update date per firm-year for the
whole ESG record (field 64), and ESG scores are refreshed in database-wide batches (Aug: 61,951
firm-years share the single date 2026-08-15). It is a snapshot date rather than a publication date, and
is used descriptively only (Figure 3).
* A monthly archive of vendor snapshots (the proposal's forward-looking extension): not started in
the assessment window. Named as the next step in the report.
* AASB S2 reports as a third channel (the proposal's hypothesis 3): the standard applies to these
firms from their 2025 financial year, so no AASB S2 report existed when the design was fixed, and
the first of them (Woodside, Rio Tinto and Santos, February 2026) arrived too late to give a panel.
The reports also bundle the emissions number with materiality statements and audited financials
(unit feedback, 21 August). Deferred.
